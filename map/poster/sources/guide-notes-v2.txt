This is the exact map geography, north up, viewed from straight above. Keep every shoreline exactly where it is and the same shape: dark teal = Pacific Ocean, lighter teal = San Francisco Bay and San Pablo Bay, tan = land. The San Francisco peninsula is at bottom center, the narrow Golden Gate strait (two black tower marks) above it, Marin between ocean and bay, the Point Reyes peninsula on the far left, the long thin Tomales Bay running from upper left down to the southeast. The cream half-dome at upper left is the glowing dome tent at Dillon Beach. The orange band with the red circle is the dusk sky with the low sun; receding ridgelines meet it. Do not move, tilt or redraw the coastline.
