Vintage 1930s WPA / National Park screen-print travel poster: a flat, top-down plan-view illustrated map of the Northern California coast from San Francisco north to Dillon Beach at dusk, north up. Only the very top of the map tilts away: the northern hills recede in three or four layered silhouette ridgelines into a dusk sky band with a low vermilion sun setting over the ocean. Flat layered inks with slight overprint misregistration and fine grain texture, limited palette only: ink #14161b, cream #efe6d4, vermilion #dd4326, deep teal #1d4b57, ochre #d6a03a, olive #6f7440. Deep teal Pacific Ocean with a few thin cream bathymetry contour lines following the coast; lighter teal San Francisco Bay and San Pablo Bay. Land in cream and ochre with olive hills (Marin hills, Mount Tamalpais, Sonoma hills) shaded in flat stepped bands, cream beaches and dunes along the shore, a few dark cypress silhouettes on the headlands. Two small ink Golden Gate Bridge towers across the narrow strait. Focal point: a large glowing geodesic dome tent on the sand dunes at Dillon Beach, triangular facets lit warm ochre from inside, with a small campfire beside it. No roads, no route lines, no text, letters, numbers, labels, compass rose, cartouche, frame, border, animals or clip-art.
