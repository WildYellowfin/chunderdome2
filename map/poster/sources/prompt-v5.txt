Vintage 1930s WPA / National Park screen-print travel poster: a flat, top-down plan-view illustrated map of the Northern California coast from San Francisco north to Dillon Beach at dusk, north up. Only the far north tilts away into three clearly separated receding ridgelines with strong atmospheric value steps: the nearest ridge dark olive, the middle ridge muted teal-grey, the farthest ridge pale hazy peach close to the sky. Above them one coherent dusk sky: a smooth screen-print gradient in three or four soft bands from warm ochre at the horizon to vermilion-orange higher up, with a large low vermilion sun half sunk into the open Pacific horizon at upper left; its glitter path of short horizontal reflection strokes lies only on the dark sea water directly beneath it, never on land. Flat layered inks with subtle overprint misregistration and a fine even grain, limited palette: ink #14161b, cream #efe6d4, vermilion #dd4326, deep teal #1d4b57, ochre #d6a03a, olive #6f7440. Deep teal Pacific; lighter teal San Francisco Bay and San Pablo Bay. Land in cream and pale ochre with olive and dark-olive hills and ridges (Marin hills, Mount Tamalpais, the Point Reyes ridge, Sonoma hills) shaded in confident flat stepped bands with crisp edges, like a carved woodblock relief map; cream beaches; a few dark cypress silhouettes on headlands. Focal point at the cream half-dome mark: a geodesic dome tent seen from above at three-quarter angle, sitting into the sand dunes of Dillon Beach, triangular facets glowing warm ochre and cream from inside, a small campfire glow beside it, a soft warm halo on the sand around it, crisp dark ink struts so the dome reads sharply. Just east of the dome a small model rocket is mid-launch from a tiny launch rod on the dunes: a slim cream-and-vermilion rocket with a bright ochre and vermilion exhaust flame, trailing a puffy cream smoke plume that curves up from the dunes high into the dusk sky. Dome and rocket in the same scale, perspective and print style as the rest of the map. No roads, no route lines, no text, letters, numbers, labels, compass rose, cartouche, frame, border, bridge, animals or clip-art.
