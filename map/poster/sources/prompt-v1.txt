Vintage 1930s WPA / National Park screen-print travel poster: a bird's-eye illustrated map of the Northern California coast from San Francisco north to Dillon Beach at dusk, north up. Flat layered inks with slight overprint misregistration and fine grain texture, limited palette only: ink #14161b, cream #efe6d4, vermilion #dd4326, deep teal #1d4b57, ochre #d6a03a, olive #6f7440. Deep teal Pacific Ocean on the left with a few thin cream bathymetry contour lines following the coast; lighter teal San Francisco Bay and San Pablo Bay on the right. Land in cream and ochre with olive and teal ridgelines (Marin hills, Mount Tamalpais, Sonoma hills) shaded in flat bands; the far northern hills recede in layers into a dusk sky band at the top with a low vermilion sun setting over the ocean. Small Golden Gate Bridge towers in ink silhouette across the strait, a few cypress silhouettes, the Point Reyes headland. A bold vermilion route line following the road. Focal point: a large glowing geodesic dome tent on the sand dunes at Dillon Beach, lit warm ochre from inside, with a small campfire beside it. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, compass rose, cartouche, border, animals or clip-art.
