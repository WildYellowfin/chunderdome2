This is the exact map geography, north up, viewed from straight above. Keep every shoreline exactly where it is: dark teal = Pacific Ocean, lighter teal = San Francisco Bay and San Pablo Bay, tan = land. San Francisco peninsula at bottom center, the narrow Golden Gate strait above it, Marin between ocean and bay, Point Reyes peninsula on the far left, the long thin Tomales Bay running from upper left to the southeast. The three bands at the top right are the receding ridgelines: dark near, mid, and pale far. Orange = dusk sky; red half circle = the sun setting at the horizon. The cream half-dome at upper left is where the glowing geodesic dome tent sits on the dunes; keep it exactly there and about that size, not over the water. Do not move, tilt or redraw the coastline.
